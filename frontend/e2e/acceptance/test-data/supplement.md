# Heat Engines and Efficiency

A heat engine converts thermal energy into mechanical work.

## Carnot Efficiency

The maximum theoretical efficiency of a heat engine operating between two
temperatures is given by the Carnot efficiency:

    eta = 1 - T_cold / T_hot

where temperatures are measured in Kelvin.

## Common Heat Engines

- Internal combustion engine: converts fuel combustion to piston motion.
- Steam turbine: uses pressurised steam to spin a rotor.
- Stirling engine: uses external heat to drive a sealed gas cycle.

## Refrigeration

A refrigerator is a heat engine run in reverse: it moves heat from a cold
reservoir to a hot reservoir by consuming work. The coefficient of
performance (COP) measures how efficiently it does this.
