# Project direction

Lyra is preparing a dependable desktop beta for students and outside contributors. The maintained
engineering queue is in Linear; public reports and proposals enter through
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/oliverdougherC/Lyra/issues). This page records priorities rather
than duplicating a task checklist that can drift from the board.

1. Protect student work through reliable saves, cancellation, recovery, backups, and updates.
2. Make model capability, processing location, and failure states understandable.
3. Validate ordinary study workflows against the packaged app and real providers.
4. Establish reproducible, signed distribution with clean-device acceptance evidence.
5. Keep onboarding, contribution guidance, and documentation current as behavior changes.

The [release evidence ledger](release-evidence.md) owns current beta blockers. The
[release process](releasing.md) owns automated app/DMG publication and updates. Historical phase
plans in the [documentation inventory](README.md) explain prior decisions; they are not the live queue
or commitments to ship future features.
